FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About HVAC

Got questions? We've answered the most common ones below.

Why does my AC struggle in the Miami heat?
Miami's heat and humidity put your AC system under serious strain — especially during peak summer months when temperatures push into the 90s. If your AC is struggling, the most common culprits are a dirty air filter, low refrigerant levels, a failing capacitor, or an aging system undersized for your cooling load. We diagnose the root cause fast so you're not stuck sweating in the Florida heat.
How often should I schedule HVAC maintenance?
In South Florida, we recommend twice-yearly maintenance — once before the heavy cooling season (spring) and once in fall. The extreme humidity and constant use means Miami AC systems wear faster than in cooler climates. Regular maintenance extends system life, improves efficiency, and prevents emergency breakdowns during the hottest months.
Do you charge extra for nights and weekends?
No. Arctic Air never charges extra for emergency calls in the evenings, on weekends, or on holidays. The price you get is the same regardless of when you need us. We believe that emergencies are stressful enough without surprise surcharges on your bill.
How do I know when to replace my AC system?
As a general rule, if your system is over 12-15 years old and requires a major repair costing more than 50% of a new system, replacement is usually the smarter investment. Signs it's time to replace: frequent breakdowns, R-22 refrigerant (phased out), skyrocketing energy bills, or uneven cooling throughout the home. Our technicians will give you an honest recommendation.
Are you licensed and insured in Florida?
Yes. Arctic Air & Plumbing is fully licensed by the State of Florida (License #CAC1234567) and carries full general liability and workers' compensation insurance on every job. You can verify our license on the Florida DBPR website at any time.
Do you provide emergency HVAC service throughout Miami?
Yes. We provide 24/7 emergency HVAC service throughout Miami-Dade and Broward County with a guaranteed 2-hour response time. Whether you're in Miami Gardens, Hialeah, Pembroke Pines, or anywhere in between — we'll be there fast.
What financing options do you offer?
We offer flexible financing options for new system installations to make it easier to get the equipment your home needs without financial stress. Ask your technician about current financing plans during your appointment — we work with several programs to fit a range of budgets.
