FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've answered the most common ones below — and we're always just a call away.

How quickly can you get to me in an emergency?
We guarantee a 2-hour emergency response time, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — including holidays. When you call, we dispatch immediately. There are no overtime charges for evenings, weekends, or holidays.
Do you charge extra for nights and weekends?
No. Arctic Air never charges extra for emergency calls in the evenings, on weekends, or on holidays. The price you get is the same regardless of when you need us.
What HVAC brands do you service?
We service and install all major HVAC brands including Carrier, Trane, Rheem, Lennox, American Standard, Daikin, Goodman, and Mitsubishi. If you have a brand not listed, just ask — we likely service it.
How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?
Our technician will perform a full diagnostic and give you an honest recommendation. As a general rule, if your system is over 12-15 years old and requires a major repair costing more than 50% of a new system, replacement is usually the smarter investment. We'll walk you through all your options with no pressure.
Are you licensed and insured in Florida?
Yes. Arctic Air & Plumbing is fully licensed by the State of Florida (License #CAC1234567) and carries full general liability and workers' compensation insurance. You can verify our license on the Florida DBPR website.
Do you offer maintenance plans?
Yes! Our maintenance plans include twice-yearly AC tune-ups, priority scheduling, discounts on repairs, and filter change reminders. Regular maintenance extends your system's life and improves efficiency, saving you money on energy bills.
What areas do you serve?
We proudly serve all of Miami-Dade and Broward County, including Miami Gardens, Hialeah, Opa-locka, North Miami, Miramar, Pembroke Pines, Carol City, Doral, Kendall, Hollywood, Aventura, and all surrounding communities.
