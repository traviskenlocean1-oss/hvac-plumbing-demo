    Questions we get a lot.

    How much does AC repair cost in Miami?

    Most repairs land between a capacitor swap and a motor replacement. The tech diagnoses first, tells you the exact price, and nothing is done until you say yes. The diagnostic fee is credited toward the repair.

    Do you offer 24/7 emergency AC repair near me?

    Yes. A real person answers day and night, and an on-call tech covers Miami-Dade and Broward for emergencies, including weekends and holidays.

    How often should an AC be serviced in Florida?

    Twice a year: once before summer and once in the fall. South Florida systems run almost all year, so cleaning the coils and clearing the drain line twice a year prevents most breakdowns.

    Should I repair or replace my AC?

    If the system is over 12 years old, uses R-22, or the repair costs more than a third of a new unit, replacing usually saves money. We show you both prices side by side.

    Do you fix water heaters, leaks and clogged drains?

    Yes. Our licensed plumbers repair and replace tank and tankless water heaters, find and fix leaks, and clear drains and sewer lines.
