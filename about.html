Our Story

Built on Reliability,
Grown by Trust

Arctic Air & Plumbing was founded in 2014 by master technician Carlos Rivera, who spent 15 years working for large HVAC corporations before deciding Miami deserved a company that put customers first — not quotas.

What started as a one-truck operation out of Miami Gardens has grown into a full-service HVAC and plumbing company with over 12 licensed technicians, serving thousands of homes and businesses across Miami-Dade County.

We built our reputation the hard way — one satisfied customer at a time — and that's still how we operate today. No pressure sales. No surprise charges. Just honest work at fair prices.
